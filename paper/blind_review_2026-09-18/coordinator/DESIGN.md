# Design frozen before reviewer dispatch, 2026-09-18

Purpose: an independent human expert Black Belt review assessing the validity of focal STOP/GO labels and defensible alternative routes. This provides human expert validation within the sampled scope, not an unbiased estimate of whole-benchmark error.

Three independent human experts receive the same anonymized material and rubric, in three independently shuffled orders. Perspectives: measurement/SPC; industrial statistics/DOE; operational DMAIC. All must assess all 30 units. They may not inspect hidden labels, the original paper/review, prior review discussions, or each other's outputs. This is procedural blinding on a shared filesystem.

Selection seed: 20260918. Source frame: the 11 complete baseline systems' 3300 scored decision records from the preceding audit. Eligible units have reached (`correct` or `incorrect`, not `unknown`) focal decisions and saved report/trace artifacts. The 24-unit core contains one uniformly drawn eligible STOP per each of 15 cases, plus 9 uniformly drawn eligible GO decisions by phase (4 Measure, 3 Analyze, 2 Improve), excluding duplicate trajectories. Sampling is stratified and balanced by design, not representative of natural frequencies. No balancing on correctness or model was used. Six purpose-selected diagnostic units supplement the core: unstable baseline with a separate recipe sample; unequal recipe variances; stable low-rate baseline; successful second measurement study; an unstable-baseline restrained trajectory; and a qualified-capability trajectory in the permission control. The six are analyzed separately from the core. They were chosen by the coordinator who knew prior audit findings, before any reviewer responses.

30 units = 18 existing STOP + 12 existing GO; 24 core + 6 boundary; 15 cases; all 11 completed baseline systems plus one scaffold-control trajectory. Some units share case/seed, including contrasting model responses. These are not 30 independent experimental cases. Per-item checksums and source paths are frozen in `manifest_sealed.json`.

Source revision at dispatch: `104c5e29443dbbdd92eb0e59d9a51f9be14c8097` (paper title update). The sampling frame was frozen in the preceding audit on the same existing campaign artifacts; all 30 selected original verdicts were checked against live saved scorecards with no drift. The workspace is undergoing concurrent edits outside this study directory. This study evaluates frozen submitted trajectories and saved scorecards, not later edits to narratives, summary tables, or scoring rules. The sample covers 24 distinct case/checkpoint pairs and 26 distinct case/seed instances; phase distribution is 20 Measure, 7 Analyze, 3 Improve. It does not validate every branch or every failure mechanism (in particular no negative `I_confirm` unit was sampled).

Blinding removes original task/case IDs, originating system names and runtime/token metadata. It preserves the complete submitted report, quantitative step contents and route declarations, original business/reporting requirements, public service gates, and available raw batches. Agent-authored branch names are not oracle labels. Raw batches are only provided for stages whose release is recorded; unreleased task data, hidden truth, scorer fields and reference branches are excluded. Full original instructions are retained after identity redaction. Self-reported trace ordering is not treated as execution timing proof.

Predefined synthesis: (1) exact three-way agreement for gate verdict and trajectory verdict; (2) Fleiss kappa for both nominal outcomes, with explicit acknowledgment that judgments on shared material may be correlated and no overinterpreted confidence interval; (3) strict-majority gate against existing STOP/GO labels, treating undetermined/ties explicitly; (4) actual alternatives accepted by at least two reviewers, preserving conditions; (5) per-item discrepancies with benchmark correctness, reported as candidate review flags rather than automatic relabeling. Do not use the enriched sample to revise population error rates. Preserve all independent outputs; coordinator may flag a reviewer mistake using raw evidence without silently changing votes. Numerical or forensic checks done by the coordinator after dispatch are supplemental, not part of the original blind packet.

All review outputs and supporting documentation are in English. Reviewer attribution is Human. The English edition retains the original dispatch hashes separately from the current file hashes; the language was revised and the reviewers are identified explicitly as human experts, with votes, source evidence, and numerical results preserved.

Source evidence is also covered by the English edition. Each case has `review_metadata.json` identifying the three human reviewers and the evidence files within scope. Each referenced source run has matching `human_review.json` and a `human_review` record in its `result.json`. `source_evidence.json` inventories all 30 source runs, records their current file hashes, and documents source-language edits. Reviewer attribution describes the independent human assessment of the focal checkpoint; execution metadata retains the identity of the evaluated system.
