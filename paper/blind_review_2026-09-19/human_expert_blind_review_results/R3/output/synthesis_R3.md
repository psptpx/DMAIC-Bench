# Independent R3 synthesis

This is an independent human expert review from a Master Black Belt perspective, emphasizing industrial decision validity, implementation, control and business claims. All 60 anonymized units were reviewed in the assigned order. The first-round judgments were preserved incrementally and were not discussed with another reviewer.

There are 22 reports with a forbidden-conclusion assertion, 5 with a forbidden-action recommendation, and 1 withholding a warranted conclusion. These categories overlap. Seven reports stopped appropriately; the other 53 reached and completed their focal discussion. These are judgments about the specified checkpoints, not whole-project grades or estimates of performance in a wider population.

The five most consequential findings are:

1. **Incorrect arithmetic can change an industrial gate even when the service accepts a declaration.** V059 reports GRR of 15.54% and eight categories, but independent crossed-ANOVA calculations give about 44.34–44.44% and fewer than three categories. V039 omits repeatability from its GRR aggregation: its 7.94% becomes about 22.4–22.8%, changing excellent-looking discrimination to conditional usability. V028 reports doubled stationary coordinates; solving the fitted gradient gives bead spacing 0.234432 and bond angle 0.240716, with maximum 48.3623 N. These discrepancies cannot be resolved by trusting a successful tool status.

2. **Null findings frequently become unsupported causal exclusions.** V016, V020, V038 and V043 move from a nonsignificant candidate comparison to claims that recipes, machines or applicators are not the explanation, sometimes naming a system-wide alternative. V060 even recommends closing the recipe branch. An inconclusive comparison can support further investigation or a redesigned experiment, but not equivalence or elimination without suitable precision and design.

3. **An unstable baseline does not prohibit every later calculation, but it limits what those calculations establish.** V011 uses a selected segment for a provisional adverse-status diagnosis and containment, while V034 and V046 use independently released monitoring windows without certifying the earlier baseline. These are defensible routes. V030 and V033 use unstable or selected-window indices more strongly. V056 dismisses a recognized pattern signal because other tests are quiet, although the tests share observations; independent checking locates its qualifying window at observations 37–39.

4. **Statistical change, causal change and business success require separate decisions.** V015 and V042 correctly retain observed improvements while refusing closure above the absolute defect-share commitment. V013 has a valid measured rate reduction but describes an unidentified intervention as having produced proven improvement. V041 appropriately recognizes response-surface inadequacy, then nevertheless recommends standardizing a coded candidate and releasing cure-temperature control without an adequately documented confirmation link. Strong observations do not supply a missing physical recipe or causal record.

5. **Excessive caution can also be wrong.** In V023, 176 breaches in 5,000 observations give a null-standard-error z of -4.801754, with two-sided normal p about 1.57e-6 and exact lower-tail p about 2.67e-7 against 5%. The report's incorrect p of 0.337 produces an unwarranted refusal of the below-target inference, under its usable classification evidence. Conversely, borderline gauges in V014 and V057 can support broad recorded-value screening while formal release and fine adjustment remain withheld.

Gate decisions are scoped to their intended use. In particular, a conditional measurement GO does not mean unrestricted acceptance of boundary dispositions or true-process tail guarantees. Isolated chart-pattern signals, the meaning of “special cause” in a cross-machine tally, and selected-window capability require judgment; the relevant entries state the interpretation and confidence. No universal textbook path was required, and a service lock was not treated as statistical ground truth.

Material checks were recomputed directly from released raw batches using Python and NumPy: counts and rates, chart point limits, continuous and attribute agreement summaries, variance components, group comparisons, selected exact binomial probabilities, regression, factorial effects and quadratic stationary solutions. Normality tests, some rank tests, prediction intervals and fitted tail probabilities were not universally reproduced; individual entries identify self-report-only quantities. A final audit added numerical corroboration and expanded quotations without changing categorical first-round judgments.

Only the assigned R3 blind directory and R3 output directory were inspected for review evidence. No repository, hidden truth, scoring code, system identity, coordinator content, other reviewer material or consensus was consulted. The coordinator supplied only a structural duplication notice. Isolation was procedural on a shared filesystem, not enforced by the operating system. Available runtime libraries were used for computation. No external sources were used, so the sources list is empty. The JSON and this synthesis are frozen as the independent first-round deliverables.
