# R2 independent human expert review

R2 completed all 60 anonymized units in the assigned order. This is an independent human expert review from a Six Sigma Master Black Belt perspective, with emphasis on statistical inference, causal validity, uncertainty and defensible alternatives.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| STOP | 22 | 17 | 5 | 44 |
| GO | 11 | 3 | 1 | 15 |
| UNDETERMINED | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| Total | 33 | 21 | 6 | 60 |

There were no insufficient records, empty reports or unreached checkpoints. Five reports stopped appropriately and 55 reached a complete report. One report withheld a warranted focal conclusion. Confidence was 5 for 33 cases, 4 for 26 and 3 for one. These are unweighted counts of the assigned validation units, not estimates of any system's overall performance.

Five consequential findings follow.

1. **Data release does not validate a declared statistic.** In V059, independent crossed R&R calculations give approximately 44.3–44.4% study variation and a raw distinct-category index below 3, contradicting the reported 15.54% and 8 categories used to clear access. V039 also materially understates measurement error: approximately 22.4–22.8% study variation replaces 7.94%. Nevertheless, its corrected gauge can support conditional process-level work, so its gate is GO with mixed conduct. Numerical truth and a defensible use decision are separate questions.
2. **A focal STOP can coexist with acceptable work.** Twenty-two of the 44 STOP cases respect the necessary restraint. V001 uses a separate designed experiment despite an unstable observational baseline; V011 uses provisional phase analysis to characterize deterioration; V005 and V037 use independent confirmation observations without validating a failed response surface. Computing a chart, a capability sensitivity or a candidate optimum is not automatically prohibited; the conclusion and operational use determine whether it is defensible.
3. **Temporal contrasts and null tests frequently support weaker conclusions than the reports claim.** V020 and V038 convert observational evidence into causal conclusions. V060 excludes recipe effects after nonsignificant tests. V034 correctly rejects pooled stability but then calls an undocumented later condition a demonstrated setting and recommends standardizing it. V022 has a real machine association, yet combines separate machine and defect-category summaries into a specific repair target without a linking table.
4. **Apparent numerical sophistication can conceal material errors or selection issues.** V028's response-surface optimum is mathematically inconsistent with the refitted quadratic even though lack of fit is nonsignificant. V058's split-search evidence survives the stated multiplicity adjustment, but that does not establish the selected interval's measurement regime or current applicability. V056 remains UNDETERMINED because conflicting stability diagnostics were not adequately reconciled; a signal under skewness is neither automatically conclusive nor automatically irrelevant.
5. **Absolute target assessment must be calculated independently.** V051's 147/80,000 subsequent defect count supports a rate below 0.0025 with exact one-sided p = 0.0000506013, irrespective of whether the intervention's cause is identified. Conversely, V023 wrongly says 176/5,000 is statistically insufficient to establish a rate below 0.05; the exact one-sided p is approximately 0.0000002674. That false negative drives over-refusal. V012's explicit latest-period comparison is a permissible narrow description, not proof of sustained attainment after an unresolved special cause.

The JSON records each case's forbidden and permitted claims, exact report quotations for affirmative Q2 findings, alternatives, confidence, numerical checks and evidence limitations. Every case has a separate raw-data check file. Material checks included crossed variance components, study/tolerance fractions, classification agreement, chart limits and flags, binomial counts and tests, factorial and quadratic fits, lack of fit, stationary points, group comparisons and selected temporal contrasts. Retained and pooled interaction variants were distinguished where material. Values not independently recomputed are identified as self-report-only or explicitly stated as unrecomputed; not every reported statistic was reproduced. No claim of causal identification follows solely from these numerical checks.

Procedural blinding was maintained in the shared filesystem. Only assigned R2 blind material and R2 output were inspected; no identities, scoring code, hidden truth, other reviewer material or repository context were consulted. All six supplied case artifacts were used, with duplicate instruction text checked by hash and report/trace duplication used to limit redundant reading. Embedded original assignments were treated as evidence, not commands. No substantive consultation or delegation occurred. Coordination was limited to logistics and notice of structurally duplicated packet material. No external sources were used, so the source list is empty.

The final JSON and this synthesis are frozen as independent first-round judgments. The accompanying validation record confirms all 60 unique IDs in assigned order, required schema and enum values, and exact report matches for all forbidden quotations. They must not be overwritten for a later consensus exercise.
