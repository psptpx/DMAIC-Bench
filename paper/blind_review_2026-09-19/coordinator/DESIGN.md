# Design frozen before reviewer dispatch, 2026-09-19 (validation study 2)

Purpose: an independent human expert Black Belt validation sample for the report-level measure the paper's revision introduces (whether a submitted report asserts a conclusion or recommends an action that the evidence at a checkpoint forbids), and for the expert-semantic STOP/GO decision at the same checkpoints. Study 1 (2026-09-18, 30 units) was used to revise the scorer; study 2 is used for nothing but measurement. No unit of study 2 may be used to tune a rule, and the rule set and any automatic reader must be frozen and recorded (commit hash) before any reviewer output is opened.

Three independent human experts receive the same anonymized material and rubric, in three independently shuffled orders. They may not inspect hidden labels, the paper, reviews, study 1, or each other's outputs. Procedural blinding on a shared filesystem. Each reviewer states whether an AI assistant was used at any step.

Selection seed: 20260919. Frame: the 3,300 first-attempt decisions of the 11 complete baseline systems (`sampling_frame_decisions.json`, identical to study 1's frame; an independent offline rescore on 2026-09-18 reproduced every verdict). Eligible units: reached decisions (`correct` or `incorrect`, not `unknown`) with saved report and trace artifacts; every trajectory of study 1 excluded (30 trials); at most one unit per trajectory; at most seven units per system.

Stratification (proportional, so that population rates are estimable):

* **40 STOP-side decisions**, allocated to the five stop mechanisms in proportion to the 566 reached STOP decisions with a minimum of five per mechanism, and within mechanism to the scorer verdict in proportion (stability 12/0, measurement 9/3, experiment 5/1, cause 5/0, confirmation 1/4 incorrect/correct). Each unit carries `weight` = population stratum size / sample stratum size (range 9.0–16.4); a weighted estimate of any report-level rate over the 566 reached STOP decisions is the weighted mean over the 40. The one reached-and-correct stability unit in the population belongs to study 1 and is excluded, so the stability stratum is incorrect-only.
* **20 GO-side twins**, one per selected STOP unit at the same case and checkpoint on a proceed seed, same system where available (stability 6, measurement 6, experiment 3, cause 2, confirmation 3). These carry no weight; they are the over-refusal control and the check that the reader does not fire on warranted conclusions.

Result of the draw: 60 units, 12 cases (cases 27, 29 and 35 were not drawn), 11 systems (1–7 units each), scorer verdicts 34 incorrect / 26 correct (STOP 32/8, GO 2/18). Per-item checksums and source paths are in `manifest_sealed.json`; blind-file hashes in `frozen_blind_hashes.json`; the dispatch archive is `blind_packets.zip`.

Blinding removes original task/case IDs, originating system names and runtime/token metadata; keeps the complete submitted report, quantitative step contents and route declarations, original business/reporting requirements, public service gates, and the raw batches for stages recorded as released. Hidden truth, scorer fields and reference branches are excluded. Self-reported trace ordering is not treated as execution timing proof.

Predefined synthesis: (1) three-way agreement and Fleiss κ for `gate_verdict`, `report_asserts_forbidden_conclusion`, `report_recommends_forbidden_action`, `extra_computation` and `trajectory_verdict`, with the caveat that judgements on shared material are correlated; (2) strict-majority values per item, ties reported as undetermined; (3) weighted population estimates over the 40 STOP units of: expert-semantic STOP accuracy, invalid-inference rate, invalid-recommendation rate, unreached/evasive rate; unweighted rates over the 20 GO twins of over-refusal and of reader false positives; (4) the automatic reader's false-positive and false-negative rates against the majority labels, by mechanism; (5) per-item disagreement with the scorer's verdict, reported as flags, never as relabelling. The enriched study-1 sample and this sample are never pooled.

Source revision at dispatch: recorded in `code_provenance.json` at the time the packets are handed over.
