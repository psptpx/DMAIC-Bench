## Business conclusion and next action

The plate and cohort are usable for the current QC-defined analysis, but this release does not support a reportable association. The immediate cause is not a failed plate or ancestry separation; it is that no QC-retained variant crossed the prespecified discovery threshold, leaving no replication candidate. Investigators should preserve the QC exclusions, analysis code, thresholds, and steward batches, and reconsider the discovery scan only under a prespecified, protocol-approved analysis change or after obtaining additional data. Any future candidate must still pass independent replication at the protocol threshold and in the same direction before it is reported.
