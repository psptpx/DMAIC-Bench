# Candidate-region case-control study report

## Where the study stands

The discovery batch contained 240 participants, 150 variants, and two affection groups. Participant QC failed for 2 participants, a failure fraction of 0.008333333333333333; this is below the protocol plate limit, so the plate passes QC and analysis can continue.

Variant QC retained 16 variants and rejected 134. The retained variants were: rs010014, rs010023, rs010028, rs010032, rs010036, rs010049, rs010052, rs010068, rs010077, rs010079, rs010092, rs010096, rs010100, rs010122, rs010133, rs010135.

## Cohort comparability

The leading structure axis separated affected and unaffected participants (p=1.180688047409278e-59). Under the protocol, separated ancestries cannot be compared. Therefore the discovery case-control comparison is not valid for reporting, and the unadjusted scan below is provisional only. The required covariate-adjusted route cannot rescue a cohort that fails the protocol's separate-ancestry prohibition.

## Discovery scan

A provisional genotype-based scan of the 16 retained variants produced 5 results below the Bonferroni threshold of 0.003125. The provisional variants were rs010028, rs010052, rs010068, rs010077, rs010135. Their unadjusted p-values and odds ratios were computed, but none is a reportable association because the ancestry gate failed.

## Replication

The steward released the replication cohort, but its candidateVariants field was empty: 0 candidates were nominated. Consequently, 0 variants replicated out of 0 candidates. There is no replication test for the provisional discovery signals, and the conclusion stops there; no association can be reported.

## Cause, change, and what it achieved

The immediate cause is ancestry separation between the affection groups, not excessive participant failure. The analysis changed from the requested direct case-control interpretation to a gated conclusion: retain QC-passing data, identify the structure failure, withhold the provisional hits, and nominate no replication candidates. This prevented a structurally confounded result from being reported.

## Next action and control

Do not report any variant from this scan. The investigators should investigate recruitment or ancestry imbalance, obtain an ancestry-overlapping cohort or redesign the comparison, and rerun the prespecified structure and association gates before nomination. Preserve the participant/variant QC thresholds, the ancestry decision, and the empty-candidate replication record as release controls for the next analysis.
