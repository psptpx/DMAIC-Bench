# Compound-expression study status

## Executive conclusion

The sequencing libraries can support a comparison: all 12 libraries passed the stated usability rules, with 6 usable control replicates and 6 usable treated replicates. The design is identifiable: the 12 usable libraries are in 1 preparation batch, and that batch contains both arms.

The sequencing analysis produced 40 genes meeting the stated call rule (adjusted p < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >= 1). However, only 10 of those nominated genes were evaluated in the independent qPCR validation batch, and 0 were confirmed. The computed concordance is 0.0, below the required 0.75. The candidate list must therefore be withheld; the panel result should be reconsidered rather than issued as a validated compound-response list.

## What was computed

- Library QC: 0 failed libraries; the minimum usable replicate count across arms was 6.
- Design: 1 preparation batch; confounding was false, because both treatment arms occur in the batch.
- Expression analysis: 40 significant genes under the released thresholds.
- Validation: 10 nominated candidates evaluated; 0 confirmed; concordance 0.0.

## Business interpretation

The study is not blocked by sequencing quality, replicate availability, or treatment/batch confounding. Those conditions were achieved without changing the library set: all libraries were retained. The limiting issue is independent validation. The sequencing signal generated a provisional list, but none of the 10 nominated candidates met the joint qPCR requirement of statistical significance and direction agreement. Consequently, no candidate list should be issued to the principal investigator as validated.

## Recommended next action

Hold the 40-gene sequencing list as provisional and do not promote the 10 nominated candidates. Reassess the panel analysis and validation design, then repeat independent validation using an appropriately powered validation experiment before issuing any candidate list. Preserve the QC, sample-sheet, counts, and validation records so that any revised analysis can be traced to the same study inputs.

## Data availability limitation

The gene-set annotation was not released because the validation-concordance gate was not met. No enrichment or gene-set interpretation was computed, so no conclusion about panel gene sets is made.
