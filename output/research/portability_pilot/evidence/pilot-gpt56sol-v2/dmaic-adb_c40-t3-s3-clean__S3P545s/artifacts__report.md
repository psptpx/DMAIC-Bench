# Compound-response study status

## Executive conclusion

The current libraries **cannot carry a compound-versus-vehicle conclusion**. Of the **12** sequenced libraries, **4** failed the facility usability rule, leaving **8** usable libraries. The control arm retained **6**, but the treated arm retained only **2**. Thus the minimum usable arm size is **2**, below the required **3**, and the replicate criterion is not met.

Under the standing rule, the study must be re-sequenced before any gene is called. No candidate list should be issued from the current data.

## What is causing the stop

Library usability was computed by applying both released criteria: at least **5000000** assigned reads and a mapping rate of at least **0.7**. There were **2** assigned-read failures and **2** mapping-rate failures, all of which reduced the treated arm to **2** usable libraries.

This is a replication failure, not evidence that the compound has no effect. The available data are insufficient to make that biological determination.

## Design and intended comparison

The sample sheet needed to evaluate treatment-versus-preparation-batch identifiability was locked after the replication gate failed. Consequently, whether the intended treatment contrast is separable from preparation batch **could not be computed**. The intended comparison cannot currently be made under the facility rules.

## Differential expression and validation

Gene-level counts were not released because the sample sheet and design assessment remain behind the failed replication gate. Therefore:

- no differential-expression model was run;
- no responding genes were identified;
- no nominated candidate set was available for independent validation; and
- validation concordance could not be computed.

These are unavailable conclusions, not negative findings. There is no defensible gene list to validate or issue.

## Required next action and control plan

Re-sequence failed treated libraries or prepare replacement treated libraries. Apply the same assigned-read and mapping-rate usability checks to every replacement, then confirm that each arm reaches at least **3** usable libraries. Only after that gate passes should the facility release the sample sheet, test whether treatment is estimable separately from preparation batch, and—if identifiable—fit the prescribed batch-aware expression model. Validation and list issuance must remain downstream of those checks.

Hold the study at the QC gate until the repeat data satisfy the rule; do not relax the thresholds or interpret the unreleased counts. A request for an existing remediation fact returned no data, so no replacement or re-sequencing result is presently available.
