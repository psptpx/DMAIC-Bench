# Study disposition: stop before association testing

## Executive conclusion
The plate is technically usable under the protocol, but this cohort cannot support a valid affected-versus-unaffected genetic association conclusion. The ancestry check shows complete separation of the phenotype groups on the leading genotype-derived axis. Do not report a variant association from this discovery cohort. This is an unidentifiable comparison, not evidence that no genetic association exists.

## Quality and retained data
Participant call rates were calculated from all delivered panel calls before variant filtering. 2 participants failed, a failure fraction of 0.008333333333333333, below the protocol's plate rejection limit. The retained cohort contains 238 participants: 119 affected and 119 unaffected. Re-genotyping the entire plate is not required by the sample-quality rule.

Variant QC was calculated after participant filtering. Minor-allele frequencies used called dosages; Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium was assessed by a conditional exact test in unaffected participants only, summing outcomes no more probable than the observed heterozygote count. There were 5 call-rate failures, 4 minor-allele-frequency failures and 3 control equilibrium failures. In total 12 variants failed and 138 were retained.

Excluded participants: P039, P197.

Excluded variants: rs010047, rs010057, rs010063, rs010072, rs010088, rs010092, rs010101, rs010118, rs010128, rs010133, rs010141, rs010149.

The complete retained participant and variant manifests follow below. `sample_qc.csv` and `variant_qc.csv` provide auditable per-record metrics and retention flags.

## Cohort comparability
Before testing any variant, retained genotypes were mean-imputed for missing calls, centered by allele frequency, and scaled by binomial genotype variance. Singular-value decomposition supplied ancestry-proxy axes. Welch tests compared phenotype groups, and group score ranges were checked for overlap across the leading axes.

The leading axis explains a variance fraction of 0.09427730354730536; its phenotype-group comparison has p = 3.654069761192594e-129. More importantly, the signed range overlap is -1.7896160275164148 (negative means a gap): affected and unaffected scores do not overlap. The protocol's ancestry-separation stop rule applies. The other leading axes do not rescue this lack of common support. Genotype PCA is a proxy for ancestry rather than a direct ancestry label; technical batch effects could also contribute, and should be investigated, but cannot justify ignoring the observed separation.

## Association and replication
No association scan, effect estimate, inflation calculation or replication test was run. Their results are not computable as valid protocol conclusions from this discovery comparison. There are no legitimately nominated discovery hits to carry into replication. Replication was not requested because the earlier comparability requirement failed; it was not denied or treated as negative replication. An independent cohort cannot retrospectively make this discovery comparison identifiable.

## Actions and control
QC filtering removed poorly called participants and variants failing the protocol criteria. It achieved a technically retained dataset, not a comparable study design. Freeze these manifests and the analysis script as the audit record. Investigate recruitment ancestry, sample provenance and processing-batch alignment with phenotype. Obtain ancestry-matched affected and unaffected participants with genuine overlap, or redesign sampling within shared ancestry strata. Re-genotyping alone will not correct ancestry-confounded recruitment.

Before resuming, repeat the protocol QC and ancestry checks on the redesigned cohort. Only if the groups are comparable should the team test variants, adjusting for overlapping structure where appropriate, apply the protocol's retained-panel multiplicity correction, and require independent same-direction replication before reporting a hit. Keep the present scan on hold; no corrective recruitment or laboratory intervention has yet been performed or shown to work.
