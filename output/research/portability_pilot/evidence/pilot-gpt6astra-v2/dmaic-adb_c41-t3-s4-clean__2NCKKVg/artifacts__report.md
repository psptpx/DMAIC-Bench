# Candidate-region association study: investigator report

## Decision
The plate is usable after protocol quality filtering, and the retained affected and unaffected participants can be compared on the measured genetic axes. **No discovery association meets the protocol's multiple-testing criterion. No variant is eligible for reporting as a replicated association.** This is a negative panel scan, not proof that the trait lacks a genetic contribution.

## Plate and cohort quality
Of the 240 discovery participants, 3 failed participant call-rate QC (failure fraction 0.0125). This is below the protocol's plate-replacement trigger: re-genotyping the entire plate is not required. Filtering retains 237 participants: 119 affected and 118 unaffected. Excluded participants: P085, P136, P193.

After participant removal, variant call rate and minor-allele frequency were recomputed on the retained cohort; Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium was tested exactly in unaffected participants. There are 11 excluded variants and 139 retained variants. The failures comprise 5 for call rate, 4 for minor-allele frequency and 2 for control Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium. These findings identify assay completeness, rarity and genotype-distribution problems; they do not establish laboratory root causes.

Excluded variants: rs040024, rs040041, rs040044, rs040059, rs040065, rs040066, rs040114, rs040124, rs040128, rs040130, rs040134.

The complete call rates, frequencies, exact equilibrium p-values and inclusion decisions are in `participants_qc.csv` and `variants_qc.csv`. No genotype was repaired or laboratory process changed: the change was exclusion under the released rules.

## Comparability before testing
PCA used retained genotypes standardized by allele-frequency variance, with missing values mean-imputed only for PCA. Group score ranges overlap on all inspected leading axes. The leading-axis affection-status comparison has p = 0.595686; the next has p = 0.664643. Their variance fractions are 0.021096 and 0.020188, respectively. There is no dominant ancestry-separating axis. A nominal difference on the fourth axis (p = 0.043790) is weak in the context of multiple axis screening and the flat leading spectrum, rather than persuasive evidence of ancestry confounding. Accordingly, an unadjusted additive trend scan was used, not a separated-ancestry comparison or a covariate-adjusted rescue of a confounded cohort.

This assessment is limited to structure detectable on this candidate panel. It cannot exclude subtle ancestry differences or establish population-wide representativeness; broader ancestry markers would strengthen a future study.

## Discovery and replication
Each retained variant was tested using an additive genotype score/trend test, omitting missing calls for that variant. The Bonferroni threshold is 0.00035971223. There are 0 significant variants. The smallest p-value is 0.01339198, for rs040143, which is not a discovery hit. Genomic inflation is 1.111377, calculated as median trend chi-square divided by its null median; this is not itself proof of confounding.

The replication cohort was released and contains 120 participants. The steward's nominated list and the independently computed discovery hit list are both empty. Consequently there are 0 replication candidates and 0 replicated associations as an empty-set accounting result. No candidate-level replication test or effect estimate was warranted. This must not be described as replication failure, successful validation of a hit, or independent confirmation of a null effect. No requested batch was denied.

## Next action and ongoing control
Report the negative corrected panel scan and preserve the full test table (`discovery_association.csv`), QC membership and scripts. Do not promote nominal signals, mine replication for substitute discoveries, or relax thresholds after seeing these results. There is no supported allele effect to take forward as a clinical finding.

Review the excluded calls and control equilibrium anomalies with the laboratory, retaining an audit of any repeat calls. If investigators need to exclude clinically meaningful effects, plan a prospectively powered, larger study and improved ancestry characterization; power and the smallest excluded effect have not been computed here. Future releases should use the same locked QC rules, ancestry comparability check, multiplicity correction and independent same-direction replication requirement. Re-run this pipeline after any documented data correction rather than manually restoring excluded records.
