# Case-control candidate-panel study report

## Executive conclusion

The plate can carry a conclusion: the computed participant failure fraction was 0.008333333333333333, below the protocol's plate re-genotyping threshold of 0.1. No plate re-genotyping is required on this QC result.

The discovery scan is not ready for an unadjusted biological conclusion. Although the QC-passing data produced 34 variants below the Bonferroni threshold of 0.00035971223021582735, phenotype groups were separated on PC1 (p=4.5567663274986146e-18) and genomic inflation was 1.8628024353056938. These findings indicate structure or confounding that must be handled before reporting the discovery list as definitive.

The nominated discovery candidate rs020008 does replicate: its replication p-value was 0.0006620796019655648, its discovery odds ratio was 3.3142678024855257, its replication odds ratio was 2.510819978944906, and the direction agreed. Thus 1 of 1 nominated candidate passed the stated replication rule.

## What was retained

The discovery batch contained 240 participants and 150 variants. 2 participants failed the individual call-rate rule, leaving 238 retained participants: 118 affected and 120 unaffected. 11 variants failed at least one variant rule, leaving 139 retained variants.

## Association and comparability

The protocol's Bonferroni threshold over retained variants was 0.00035971223021582735. The unadjusted additive trend scan identified 34 significant variants. This cannot be treated as a final association conclusion because PC1 separation was significant at p=4.5567663274986146e-18 and lambdaGC was 1.8628024353056938. The case and control groups are therefore not demonstrably comparable on this cohort without adjustment.

## Replication

The nominated candidate rs020008 had a discovery odds ratio of 3.3142678024855257 and a replication odds ratio of 2.510819978944906; the signs agreed and the replication p-value was 0.0006620796019655648. This supports reporting rs020008 as replicated under the protocol's direction and p-value rule, while not resolving the discovery-wide stratification concern.

## Recommended next steps and control plan

Re-run the discovery association with adjustment for the measured structure (including appropriate principal components or other prespecified covariates), then reassess calibration and multiplicity using the retained data. Do not promote the full unadjusted discovery list to a biological claim. Preserve the QC filters, retained-participant and retained-variant lists, analysis code, and the exact protocol thresholds as the held process. Keep the independent replication result attached to the nominated candidate and seek additional independent validation after the adjusted analysis.
