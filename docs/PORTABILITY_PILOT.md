# Second-domain portability pilot: design

Answers the review question "how feasible is it to expand DMAIC-Bench beyond Six Sigma to a
second domain with well-specified preconditions?" with an experiment rather than an argument.
Two life-sciences workflows were written against the unchanged v2 contract (`V2Case`,
guarded decision graph, data steward, separate verifier, the same six scoring axes), built as
their own Harbor dataset, and run with three contestants from the main comparison.

Results: `docs/RESULT_PORTABILITY_PILOT.md` (generated by
`scripts/pilot_portability_analysis.py`). Per-trial evidence, anonymised:
`output/research/portability_pilot/evidence/` (`scripts/pilot_export_release.py`).

## What was reused, what was added

| layer | reused unchanged | added |
|---|---|---|
| decision graph, oracle walk, PQ / SC / NF / MS scoring | yes | - |
| steward, locks over declared values, separate verifier image | yes | five public gate predicates in `spec.PUBLIC_GATES` and their guard atoms in `v2_tasks` |
| method vocabulary | 50 quality-engineering methods | 14 methods in four families: `qc`, `design`, `expression`, `gwas` (`dmaic_bench/taxonomy.yaml`) |
| reference implementations | `truth/verifiers.py` | `truth/omics.py`, registered into `REFERENCE_IMPLS` |
| oracle solution (`solve.sh`) | walk, guard parser, declare protocol | fourteen `m_*` functions mirroring `omics.py` |
| instruction template | every rule and the report contract | domain nouns swapped for `industry == life_sciences` (`build.DOMAIN_WORDING`) |
| case registry | 15 DMAIC cases | `v2/pilot_cases/`, discovered only under `DMAIC_PILOT_DOMAINS=1` |

The 97-task corpus, its held-out split, `shape_tiers.json` and every count-pinning test are
untouched by default: the pilot is opt-in at discovery time and is built to a separate
dataset directory with its own verifier base image (`ghcr.io/dmaic-bench/verifier:0.2.0-pilot-base`).

## The two cases

### adb_c40 -- compound response on a targeted expression panel (bulk RNA-seq)

Reconstructed from two published failures: the mouse ENCODE comparison whose sequencing batch
was confounded with species (Lin et al. 2014; Gilad & Mizrahi-Man 2015) and the partially nested
design of the ComBat-seq application (GSE83083 and GSE59765, controls across batches, each
treatment in one batch). Data are simulated (400 genes, 6 + 6 libraries).

Preconditions, stated in the brief the steward releases first (the numbers are the case's own
settings, taken from the low end of published guidance):

* a library is usable at >= 5 M assigned reads and >= 70% mapping; each arm keeps >= 3 usable
  replicates before any gene is called (Conesa et al. 2016 give three as the minimum, five
  million mapped reads and 70-90% mapping as reference points; Schurch et al. 2016 recommend at
  least six replicates, so three is a floor);
* the treatment contrast must be estimable separately from preparation batch -- a design in
  which every batch holds one arm is not full rank (DESeq2 vignette; Leek et al. 2010);
* with more than one batch, batch is a term in the model.

| seed | shape | route | correct action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | single-batch-signal | A-proceed | two-arm comparison; ~40 genes; qPCR validates; enrichment |
| 4 | single-batch-null | A-proceed | two-arm comparison; nothing responds; stop there |
| 2 | batched-signal | B-divert | batch in the model; the batch-blind comparison over-calls by >= 10 genes; validates; enrichment |
| 5 | batched-null | B-divert | batch in the model; nothing responds |
| 1 | confounded-batches | C-stop | every batch holds one arm: no contrast; the batch-blind comparison "finds" ~150 genes |
| 3 | too-few-replicates | C-stop | four treated libraries fail the read rules; re-sequence |
| 6 | confounded-after-qc | C-stop (trap) | the PLANNED sheet is balanced and replicates pass (4 >= 3), but the four failed libraries are the two treated in L1 and the two controls in L2: among usable libraries every batch holds one arm |
| 7 | unvalidated-calls | C-stop (trap, late) | the panel calls ~40 genes convincingly; the independent qPCR confirms none of the ten nominated; the list is withheld after four analyses |

The go routes are five analyses long (library QC, design, comparison, validation, enrichment).

Locks are ENFORCED as in the main corpus: the sample sheet is held until `min_replicates >= 3`
is declared, the counts until `design_confounded == false` is declared, the gene sets until
`validation_concordance >= 0.75` is declared. The steward checks the declaration, never the
truth.

### adb_c41 -- candidate-region association in a case-control cohort (GWAS QC)

Protocol thresholds follow Anderson et al. 2010 and Marees et al. 2018; structure correction
follows Price et al. 2006. Data are simulated (240 participants, 150 variants, 40 of them
ancestry-informative; one causal variant at OR ~ 3 on the signal seeds).

| seed | shape | route | correct action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | homogeneous-signal | A-proceed | unadjusted trend scan; one hit; replicates; per-allele effect |
| 4 | homogeneous-null | A-proceed | unadjusted trend scan; none; stop there |
| 2 | stratified-signal | B-divert | scan with PC1 + PC2 as covariates; one hit (the unadjusted scan calls >= 6); replicates; effect |
| 5 | stratified-null | B-divert | adjusted scan; none |
| 1 | separated-ancestry | C-stop | cases and controls occupy disjoint ancestry on PC1: no comparison |
| 3 | plate-failure | C-stop | 13% of participants below the call-rate rule: re-genotype |
| 6 | separated-on-pc2 | C-stop (trap) | a balanced recruitment-site split owns PC1 (status p = 0.47); the status-aligned ancestry split is disjoint on PC2 |
| 7 | unreplicated-hit | C-stop (trap, late) | a genome-wide-significant discovery hit that the independent cohort does not reproduce; not reported, after four analyses |

The go routes are five analyses long (sample QC, variant QC, structure, scan, replication, effect).

The discovery stages all read the one genotype batch, so no lock can be enforced on them (a
batch cannot be held against a value computed from itself), and the replication batch is left
unenforced on purpose: every fork in this case measures unenforced self-restraint, the regime
of the four unlocked tasks the paper reports apart. Adjusting for the leading components
on a homogeneous cohort is accepted at the structure fork (`accepted_methods`), since Price et
al. recommend it routinely.

## Why eight seeds and five-analysis routes

The first build (v1: seeds 0-5, two forks per task, jobs `pilot-*`) was run for GPT-6-astra
and GPT-5.6-sol under the protocol brief. GPT-6-astra took every one of its 66 forks
correctly, compared with its 24% three-attempt mean no-go accuracy under the plain instruction
on DMAIC. Reading its trajectories gave
three reasons, none of them a defect: the brief states the rule and its consequence; the no-go
seeds are extreme and unambiguous; the stop comes one or two analyses in. The extended build
answers each: the thresholds-only brief (below) removes the stated consequence; seeds 6 add
the corpus's hardest shape, a headline check that passes while a second criterion fails; seeds
7 put the stop at the end of a five-analysis project, after a convincing result, where the
corpus's confirmation forks sit. Jobs `pilot-*-v2` and `pilot-*-v2-bare`.

## Validation before spending

* generator acceptance loops force every seed onto its intended route and make each trap real
  (`python -m dmaic_bench.v2.pilot_cases.rnaseq_batch` / `gwas_qc` print the truths);
* `correspondence.check_case`: no findings on either case (C1-C7);
* `harbor/tests/test_v2_build.py`, `test_v2_preconditions.py`, `test_oracle_mirrors_guard_dsl.py`
  under `DMAIC_PILOT_DOMAINS=1`: pass, except the probe-seed pin (the pilot is not built with
  honesty probes);
* `harbor/scripts/e2e.sh` on every built task of the sixteen-task build: the oracle scores
  reward 1.000 with pq = nf = 1.0 on all 16, and zero truth values are reachable in any agent
  image (`output/research/portability_pilot/e2e-v2/summary.txt`).

## One scorer fix, measured on the pilot

`verify.py` dropped boolean outputs from a trace ("a bool is not a measurement"). The first
scored GWAS trials showed what that costs on an indicator truth: GPT-6-astra computed the
ancestry check correctly, wrote `separated: false`, and was scored `no numeric key` on that
truth (NF 5/6), while the self-consistency re-walk, blind to the field, took the stop branch on
every go seed (SC 0.5). Booleans are now coerced to 0/1 (`verify.py`, `_prune`). Measured on
the main corpus: no boolean truth exists there, and re-scoring `gpt6astra-97` under the old and
the new scorer gives identical rewards on every trial. Pilot trials scored inside the container
before the fix are re-scored with `scripts/rescore.py --dataset harbor/datasets/portability-pilot
--apply`; the analysis reads `verifier-rescored/` where it exists.

## Campaigns

`scripts/run-pilot-v2-campaigns.sh` (after `run-pilot-v2-orchestrate.sh` validated the trap
tasks): GPT-6-astra, GPT-5.6-sol, GPT-5.6-luna in sequence on the protocol brief (k = 3), then
on the thresholds-only brief (k = 1); terminus-2 scaffold, plain instruction, 4 concurrent
trials; jobs `harbor/jobs/pilot-*-v2` and `pilot-*-v2-bare` (plus `pilot-gpt56sol-v2-bare-fill`
for one trial lost to a gateway 524). Provider `openai-gateway`, the same gateway as the 97-task
runs. 192 trials, 552 graded forks, 2026-09-23. Every trial is re-scored against
`harbor/datasets/portability-pilot-v2r{,-bare}` (identical data; carries the boolean fix and the
over-work declaration) and the analysis reads `verifier-rescored/`. The first-round build
(v1: 12 tasks, GPT-6-astra and GPT-5.6-sol) is not reported separately; its evidence sits
beside the rest under `output/research/portability_pilot/evidence/` (jobs `pilot-gpt6astra`,
`pilot-gpt56sol`), and `output/research/portability_pilot/README.md` explains the job names.

## Result in one table

| system | brief | no-go | divert | go | own numbers said stop, went anyway |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-6-astra | protocol, k=3 | 22/24 | 12/12 | 102/102 | 2/2 |
| GPT-5.6-sol | protocol, k=3 | 23/24 | 11/12 | 96/102 | 1/1 |
| GPT-5.6-luna | protocol, k=3 | 17/24 | 6/12 | 90/102 | 6/7 |
| GPT-6-astra | thresholds-only, k=1 | 6/8 | 3/4 | 33/34 | 2/2 |
| GPT-5.6-sol | thresholds-only, k=1 | 6/8 | 3/4 | 32/34 | 2/2 |
| GPT-5.6-luna | thresholds-only, k=1 | 5/8 | 1/4 | 31/34 | 3/3 |

Three readings, with the caveat that two graphs and three agents cannot rank the factors:

1. The paradigm ports: the oracle finishes every task, the locks and the verifier work
   unchanged, and the traps discriminate: both RNA-seq trap no-go forks are passed on 9/9
   attempts across agents; the GWAS PC2 and replication traps are passed on 7/9 and 8/9.
2. The DMAIC failure rates do not port unchanged: no-go accuracy across three attempts is
   22/24, 23/24 and 17/24 for GPT-6-astra, GPT-5.6-sol and GPT-5.6-luna, compared with
   three-attempt means of 24%, 22% and 25%, respectively, under the plain instruction on DMAIC.
   Where agents fail they fail the corpus's way -- nine of ten wrong no-go forks come after
   the agent's own numbers implied the stop.
3. The brief control helps interpret the gap. On first attempts, removing the stated
   consequence lowers GWAS structure no-go accuracy from 4/6 to 0/6; RNA-seq no-go accuracy
   remains 12/12 under both briefs. GPT-5.6-luna fails almost only in the unlocked GWAS case and
   is near-perfect in the locked RNA-seq case -- the pattern of the paper's four unlocked tasks.

## The brief control

The protocol brief the steward releases first states each rule AND what a failed rule forbids
("a design in which every batch holds one arm supports no contrast"; "participants drawn from
separate ancestries cannot be compared"). That is the regime of the paper's *normative*
instruction, not its plain one, and the first campaign showed why it matters: GPT-6-astra,
23% first-attempt no-go accuracy on DMAIC under the plain instruction, took every one of 66
forks correctly here. So the same twelve tasks are built a second time with a thresholds-only
brief (`DMAIC_PILOT_BRIEF=thresholds`, dataset `portability-pilot-bare`): the same numbers
(read and mapping minima, replicate minimum, call-rate, MAF, HWE and Bonferroni thresholds,
the 10% plate rule) and nothing about consequences. Whether a confounded design supports a
contrast, or a two-ancestry cohort a comparison, is then the contestant's own discipline to
know -- the situation of the plain instruction in DMAIC. Run at k = 1 for the three systems
(`scripts/run-pilot-bare-campaigns.sh`, jobs `pilot-*-bare`) after the protocol campaigns.

## Reading the result

The pilot tests whether the EVALUATION PARADIGM transfers: can a second domain's preconditions
be written as guards over computed diagnostics, can the steward and verifier grade them, does
the oracle finish. Whether the failure rates observed in DMAIC persist is an empirical question
the pilot can only sketch: 16 tasks, 2 case graphs, 3 systems. Per-fork counts are reported with
their denominators; nothing here is a population estimate.
