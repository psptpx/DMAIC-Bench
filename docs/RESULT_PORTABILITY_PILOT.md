Two life-sciences decision-graph cases, eight seeds each (sixteen tasks), three OpenAI-family agents, three attempts under the protocol brief and one under the thresholds-only brief, the same scaffold and scorer as the 97-task DMAIC corpus. Generated by `scripts/pilot_portability_analysis.py`; the per-trial evidence (scorecards, trace, report, steward audit) is in `output/research/portability_pilot/evidence/`, anonymised by `scripts/pilot_export_release.py`.
