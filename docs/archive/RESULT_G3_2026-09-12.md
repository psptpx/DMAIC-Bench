# G3 — the controls bound the scale, on the corpus that now ships

**oracle 109/109 at 1.000. nop 109/109 at 0.000.** 97 clean tasks + 12 honesty probes,
containerised, agent images and verifier images built offline.

This is the first containerised G3 since the corpus grew to 97 and the first EVER for the
honesty probe. `docs/EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` carried "the containerised G3 has **not** been re-run
— that remains owed before any campaign" for the whole of the preceding sequence. It is paid.

## What it cost to get here, and what that says

Two runs were lost whole (312 trials) to the host, not to the benchmark: every buildx builder
here uses the `docker-container` driver and cannot see the local image store, so even a present
base image resolves over the registry; and the host's egress proxy is not inherited by a build
container, so `pip install` fails inside the image build while the host reaches PyPI. Both are
closed by `scripts/build-verifier-base.sh` plus `DOCKER_BUILDKIT=0`; see `harbor/README.md`.

Then the run that completed found **three defects that no amount of reading would have found**:

| what | how it showed | why it survived three earlier G3 runs |
|---|---|---|
| a batch locked behind a declaration nothing could make | `adb_c38` oracle 0.706, the audit log showing two refusals | the gate derivation that emits it landed in P0, AFTER the last G3 |
| `m_one_sample_t` testing every process against ZERO | the column probe not disabling it | masked by the data: a mean far from both the target and zero underflows the p-value either way |
| the truth package trimming its numbers but not its evidence table | 3 of 12 probes at pq 0.75-0.8, every other axis perfect | the probe had never run containerised at all |

The middle one is the one to remember. It was not introduced by any recent change; it had always
been there, and it passed because the corpus happened not to contain a process sitting near its
target. A green G3 is evidence about the seeds that exist, not about the code.

## The probe, and why there are only 12

The probe withholds ONE COLUMN (or a whole batch where every node reading it is off the guard
path), and 12 of 97 seeds can carry one. That ceiling is structural: **80% of the
method-running nodes in this corpus produce a field some guard reads**, because in a
data-conditioned decision graph the analyses ARE the routing. Withhold what an analysis needs
and you either move the route or erase the evidence Path Quality infers the branch from. The
probe lives in the 20% that is terminal, and of that, the part with a column that disables one
analysis and no other.

## Re-run debt

**37 of the 97 clean tasks differ from the build the GLM campaign measured** — 35 changed and 2
are new — across `adb_c32`, `adb_c34`, `adb_c35`, `adb_c37`, `adb_c38`. Most of it is the P0
gate derivation, which changed what the steward enforces and therefore the difficulty. A report
spliced from two builds that differ in gate enforcement is not one result. The 95-task
GLM-5.3-Flash note that reported such a splice was marked superseded and has been removed;
the 97-task run on one build is `docs/archive/RESULT_GLM53FLASH_97.md`.
